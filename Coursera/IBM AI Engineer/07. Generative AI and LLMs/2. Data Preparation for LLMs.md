# 2 Data Preparation for LLMs

## Prepare Data

### Tokenization

- Breaking a sentence into smaller pieces

Tokenization methods

- Word-based
  - Text divided into individual words
  - Preserves semantic meaning
  - Increase vocabulary
- Character-based
  - Split text into characters
  - Smaller vocabulary
  - May not convey same information as words
  - Increase input dimensionality
- Subword-based
  - Frequently used words unsplit
  - Infrequent words broken down
  - Algorithms
    - WordPiece
      - Evaluates benefits and drawbacks of splitting and merging two symbols
    - Unigram
      - Break text into smaller pieces
      - Narrow down a list of possibilities base on frequency of appearance
    - SentencePiece
      - Segment text into manageable parts and assign IDs
