## Positional Encoding, Attention and Application in Classification

### Positional Encoding

- Information about position of each embedding
- Consists of series of sine and cosine waves
- `pos` represents position of sine wave
- `i` dimension index generate unique sine or cosine wave for each embedding
- Position encoding can be learnable parameters
- Segment embedding provide additional positional information
